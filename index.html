Implementation notes

Audio uses source analysis plus a deterministic timeline dataset: exact weighted scene categories are scattered by a seeded Feistel permutation, neighboring scenes share hierarchical macro-context, and smoothly crossfaded regimes alter modal focus, spectral balance, stochastic texture, stereo width, modulation and event density. A deterministic additive spectral field superposes many independently seeded frequency components with separate magnitudes, phases, stereo phase offsets and slow drift; each scene reshapes those per-frequency amplitudes using exact weighted classes while preserving bounded total power. Audio also adds a multi-component source-spectrum layer: a 24-band analysis timeline uses soft time/frequency median masks and deterministic KL-NMF to discover latent sustained components plus transient and diffuse layers. Exact ratio masks preserve measured source power per band and frame, while each layer receives independent filtered carriers, temporal dynamics, micro-modulation, and stereo covariance. The two raw noise layers are normalized and combined by power share before source-relative level planning and peak-safe mixing. Every generation also emits the exact post-gain noise-only track. Video currently uses the established environment synthesizer with browser-native real-time capture and MediaRecorder; current Chromium browsers are recommended.
